# Roadmap

What we plan to work on after 26.11.0. There are no dates.

- Each level starts when monthly sponsorship reaches it. The
  [Sponsor page](https://github.com/sponsors/varunnjbehera) shows how close it is.
- Within a level, the order follows what people ask for in Issues, so any line can move.
- Ideas and votes are welcome in [Issues](https://github.com/justanothermedialab/miserly/issues).

## Now

- The Microsoft Store copy, signed by Microsoft and kept up to date by the Store.

## At $20 a month

This pays for a basic AI plan, the tools used to write and check the code: enough to keep Miserly
maintained.

- Fixes for problems reported in Issues.
- Security updates to the packages Miserly uses.
- New versions that keep working on new Windows, Node.js and Docker releases.

## At $100 a month

This pays for a larger AI plan, enough for new features.

**Installing and updating**

- A signed download on GitHub, so Windows stops warning about it.
- An installer beside the zip, then updates from inside the app.
- Faster pages on small home servers with many years of history.

**Valuing what you hold**

- Mutual funds, stocks and exchange rates valued from public price files, downloaded whole and
  looked up on your computer. Nothing about you is sent out, and a value you type still wins.
- Interest worked out for fixed and recurring deposits, PPF and EPF, and a loan's balance with
  each EMI split into principal and interest, from the rate and dates you type. Deposits that
  mature on their date, and PPF's yearly limit checked. Shown as estimates.

**Reports and charts**

- A balance line on each section page.
- The net worth table by quarter or by year.
- A spending calendar for the month.
- One category's history by month, with each year's total and average.
- Income by source.
- What was left each month, as a line over the income and spending bars.
- Reports filtered by account or by category.
- This month against the same month last year.

**Everyday use**

- Undo after changing a field in place.
- Importing from an Actual Budget file in a language other than English.

**Several people on one server**

- A second admin.
- A download of the whole data file, for the person who set up the server.
- A sign-in lockout that holds across a restart.
- Removing a removed member's data for good.

## At $200 to $500 a month

The largest AI plans, for the bigger pieces of work.

**Money and accounts**

- Linking a loan to what it paid for, such as a home loan to the property.
- Exchange rates by date, so past months convert at their own rate.
- Changing the currency for totals after setup.
- Seeing net worth in another currency for a moment.
- Changing an account's currency or type after it is made.
- Credit card statements: the billing cycle and the due date.
- More Indian account types: Sukanya Samriddhi, NSC, SCSS, ULIP and others. Today they go in as
  Other asset.
- Encrypting the data file.

**Bringing data in**

- Imports from YNAB, Cashew and Money Manager exports.

**Everyday use**

- Tags on transactions.
- Changing the date or the account of many transactions at once.
- Allocation over time.
- Report pages you save and arrange yourself.
- Reordering sections and accounts in the sidebar.
- Right-click menus on Payees and Categories.

**Several people on one server**

- Invite links, instead of the admin making each login.
- A view-only way to share an account.
- Who owes whom between members.
- Handing an account over to another member.
- Hiding a payee for yourself only.
- Categories only the admin can change, and a label on each row for whose spending it is.

## Machines, bought once

Each needs a machine to build and test on, so each waits until sponsorship has paid for it.

- An Apple silicon Mac, for a macOS version, with Apple's developer fee ($99 a year) to sign it.
- ARM64 machines, to test the Docker image on ARM servers and a Windows on ARM version.
- An Android phone, for a layout that works in phone browsers on the self-hosted version.
- A second monitor at another display scale, to test the window on both.
- A development machine with more memory: the current one has 8 GB and can't run the full browser
  test suite.

## Not planned

- Reading your bank, SMS, email or statements, or linking to a bank or a broker. What you type is
  the record.
- Envelope budgeting.
