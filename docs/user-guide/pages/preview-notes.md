## Not in this version

- Reading your bank statements, SMS or email, or linking to a bank or broker. That's by design:
  you type what you want tracked. The one import is Actual Budget's own export (see
  [Coming from Actual](switching-from-actual.md)).
- Prices: fund NAVs, stock prices and gold rates aren't fetched. You type a value with
  **Update value**.
- Splitting one transaction across several categories. An imported Actual split comes in as
  separate rows.
- Budgets, scheduled or repeating transactions, and reconciling against a statement.
- Changing the currency for totals or the month the year starts in after setup.
- Small savings schemes other than PPF (Sukanya, NSC, KVP, SCSS, Post Office): add them as
  Other asset.
- A phone app, or the browser version on a phone. Miserly is for Windows and desktop browsers.

## Known issues

- In a small window, the month-by-month tables in Reports can be wider than the page: scroll
  them sideways, or widen the window.
- Windows warns about an unknown publisher the first time you open it (see
  [Install](install.md)).

## Reporting a problem or an idea

In Miserly, open **⋯**, **About Miserly**, and click **Report a problem**: it opens the issue
page on GitHub. The most useful report has:

- what you did, step by step,
- what you expected, and what happened instead,
- a screenshot (Windows key + Shift + S),
- your Miserly version: the copy button beside it in **About Miserly** copies the version and
  your browser or app details.

Please don't attach your `miserly.db` file or a screenshot with real figures unless you're happy
for anyone to see them: GitHub issues are public.
