One copy of Miserly can serve a household. If you're the only person using it, you can skip
this page.

## Logins

The person who created the first login is the admin. In **Settings**, under **Members**, the
admin adds people with **Add member**: a username, a display name and a temporary password. Each
person chooses their own password the first time they sign in.

Each person sees only their own accounts, plus any account someone has shared with them. The
admin manages logins but can't see anyone else's accounts.

### If you forget your password

Ask the admin. They click **Reset password** beside your name under **Members** and set a
temporary password, and you choose a new one the next time you sign in.

The admin can't open your accounts from their own login. But a reset is a way in, and it's
worth knowing plainly: whoever sets the temporary password can use it to sign in as you, before
you do, and see what you see. So a reset is never hidden from you. Your old password stops
working, you're signed out everywhere, and after you sign in with the temporary one and pick
your own, the top of every page says the admin reset your password, and on which day, until you
close it. If you see that and didn't ask for a reset, talk to your admin.

The admin's own password has no one to reset it. Write it down.

## Sharing an account

Open the account and click **Share** at the top. Pick a person under **Share with** and click
**Share**. They hold it from today: everything from then on is theirs to see and change too,
and the rows before that stay yours, shown to them as one line, **Balance when you joined**. You
stay its owner. Only the owner shares, removes people and changes the split.

A shared account is split equally between the people who hold it unless you set a split: click
**Set split**, type each person's percentage (they must add up to 100), pick the date it starts
from, and click **Save**. Earlier dates keep the split they had. Each person's net worth counts
their share. Under the dashboard's net worth, **Shared with others** gives the full amount and
your share, and opens a list of each shared account.

To stop sharing, open the same panel and click the cross beside the person. They lose access
from today: the account stays on their All accounts page under **Show closed**, as **No longer shared with you**,
showing what it held on their last day.

### What the other holder reads

What you type on a row of a shared account, the other holder reads: its category, its payee and
its note. That holds for a transfer too. Move money from a joint account into an account of your
own and the other holder sees the row in the joint account with its amount, its category and its
note, but not the account it went to. A category such as "PPF deposit" or a note such as "Rahul's
school fees" can still say where the money went, so pick words you're happy for them to read.
Their reports count that row by its category, as yours do.

### Payee names are one list

Payee names are one list for everyone in your group, so the same shop or person is spelled the
same way for all of you. You see only the payees used on your own accounts. One thing does show
through: if you type a name that someone else in the group already uses, Miserly treats it as
that payee, so it keeps their spelling and doesn't appear as new in your list. That can tell you
the name is in use, though not who uses it, on which account or for how much. If a payee's name
is something you'd rather keep to yourself, such as a doctor or a lawyer, a shortened or private
nickname avoids it.
